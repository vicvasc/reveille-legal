Reveille privacy policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Reveille is a morning-photo app. You share a photo each morning with small, private circles of people you chose. This page explains what we keep and what we don't do with it.

What we store

  • Your email
  • Your display name
  • Your morning photos and captions
  • The dates you post
  • The circles you belong to
  • Reports you file
  • People you've blocked

Where it lives and who can see it

Your data is stored with Supabase, in the US East region.

Your photos are private. They are only visible to members of your circles, and only on days you've also posted.

What we don't do

  • No ads.
  • We don't sell your data.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking.

Reminders

The morning reminder is a notification scheduled on your phone. We don't use a push notification server.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the app, in the account section at the bottom of the Archive tab. This removes your profile, posts and photos right away.

Age

You must be at least 13 to use Reveille.

Contact
